The new era of AI

often referred to as the "AI Revolution" or "Age of Artificial Intelligence,
" is characterized by rapid advancements in AI technology and its widespread adoption across various sectors.
This era is marked by new types of AI, like Generative AI,
and the ability to leverage previously inaccessible unstructured data.
It also involves the evolution of AI in terms of ethics, regulation, and its impact on society and business.

Generative AI:

Generative AI, powered by algorithms like LLMs (Large Language Models), is
